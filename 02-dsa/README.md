## What this section develops

- Understanding time and space complexity through Big O notation.
- Choosing appropriate data structures for different problems.
- Designing algorithms from clear and repeatable steps.
- Comparing different solutions and their trade-offs.
- Implementing fundamental algorithms from scratch in Python.
- Improving solutions through testing, measurement, and refactoring.
- Recognizing patterns used in technical interviews and real systems.

## Learning method

Each topic is studied through four stages:

1. **Understand** the idea and the problem it solves.
2. **Implement** the solution from scratch.
3. **Analyze** its time and space complexity.
4. **Compare** it with alternative approaches.

## Engineering mindset

The objective is not to memorize solutions. It is to understand why an algorithm works, recognize when it should be used, and explain its strengths and limitations clearly.
